* ## Budget Optimizer

- **Objective:** Maximize expected conversions within the available budget.
- **Inputs:** Customer segments, treatment effects, and campaign costs.
- **Constraint:** Total campaign spending must not exceed the allocated budget.
- **Output:** Recommended customer allocation and expected conversions.
